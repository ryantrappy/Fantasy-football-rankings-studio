# Manager personalities

Transaction activity counts unique transaction IDs separately per manager, season and type. A multi-player trade counts once per participating manager. Only declared provider categories receive numeric counts; absent categories are unsupported, never zero. Coverage includes observed weeks and complete/partial status. Waiver claims are unsupported unless distinguished by provider evidence.

Activity labels are opt-in. Complete coverage of at least four weeks is required: two or more pickups plus drops per covered week is Tinkerer; below two is Steady hand. Historical score and lineup samples cannot establish transaction completeness.
