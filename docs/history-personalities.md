# Manager personalities

Transaction activity counts unique transaction IDs separately per manager, season and type. A multi-player trade counts once per participating manager. Only declared provider categories receive numeric counts; absent categories are unsupported, never zero. Coverage includes observed weeks and complete/partial status. Waiver claims are unsupported unless distinguished by provider evidence.

Activity labels are opt-in. Complete coverage of at least four weeks is required: two or more pickups plus drops per covered week is Tinkerer; below two is Steady hand. Historical score and lineup samples cannot establish transaction completeness.

Player impact uses reciprocal completed matchup scores, explicit lineup availability, and a starter ID appearing in that week's ownership list. A guardian angel appears in a win; a nemesis appears on the opposing team in a loss. Decisive contributions exceed the margin of victory (removing their points changes the outcome). This assumes no replacement player and does not establish causation. Duplicate starter IDs count once. Fewer than three appearances is flagged. Missing rosters and unmatched fixtures are excluded and counted; provider identity changes stay separate.

Ownership counts unique observed year/week roster entries, not interpolated ownership between snapshots. Drafts and transaction acquisitions retain separate deduplicated event counts. Favorites use observed weeks, seasons and repeated drafts in that order; acquisition-only records are labeled candidates. Manager filtering exposes the player relationship directly. Neither acquisition history nor missing weeks claims complete career ownership.

Draft overlap compares unique selected provider player IDs within the same declared draft format (snake, linear or auction), reporting both managers' observed seasons and the overlap/union counts. It is not an intent model. Unknown formats are unsupported, and different formats are not pooled. Queue steals are unsupported because current providers do not supply explicit historical queues; next-pick adjacency never proves intent.

Archetypes are independent and may overlap or be absent. Tinkerer requires at least two fully covered seasons with four observed weeks each and at least two pickups plus drops per week. Waiver Hawk requires ten rated pickups and at least 60% positive lift (free agents included; waiver subtype unsupported). Eternal Runner-Up requires three known final-placement seasons and two second-place finishes. Trophy Collector requires three known championship seasons and two titles. Every emitted label includes its metric, sample, seasons and threshold. No unsupported playoff-causality label is inferred from championship counts.
